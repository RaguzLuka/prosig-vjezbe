SVRHA VJEŽBE je pratiti jednu miozinsku glavu kroz ciklus poprečnog mosta (vezanje ATP → odvajanje → hidroliza i napinjanje → vezanje na aktin → zaveslaj → rigor) i shvatiti ulogu Ca²⁺, troponina, tropomiozina i ATP-a — te što se događa kad ATP-a nema (rigor mortis, ishemija) ili kad Ca²⁺ curi bez prestanka (maligna hipertermija, dantrolen).
PURPOSE OF THE EXERCISE: to follow one myosin head through the cross-bridge cycle (ATP binding → detachment → hydrolysis and cocking → attachment to actin → power stroke → rigor) and to grasp the roles of Ca²⁺, troponin, tropomyosin and ATP — and what happens when ATP is missing (rigor mortis, ischaemia) or Ca²⁺ leaks without end (malignant hyperthermia, dantrolene).
