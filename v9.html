SVRHA VJEŽBE je (1) vidjeti što se u samom kanalu događa tijekom akcijskog potencijala — Na⁺-kanal s dvoja vrata i tri stanja (zatvoren, otvoren, inaktiviran) nasuprot K⁺-kanalu s jednim vratima i dva stanja — i (2) razumjeti zašto potencijal mirovanja, ionski gradijenti i volumen stanice ovise o Na⁺/K⁺-crpki (ouabain, cijanid).
PURPOSE OF THE EXERCISE: (1) to see what happens inside the channel during an action potential — the Na⁺ channel with two gates and three states (closed, open, inactivated) versus the K⁺ channel with one gate and two states — and (2) to understand why the resting potential, the ion gradients and the cell volume depend on the Na⁺/K⁺ pump (ouabain, cyanide).
